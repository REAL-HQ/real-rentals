- Stripe Match: the owner/manager report in stripe-match.functions.ts lists live Stripe customers (list/retrieve only) and matches them to applications by stripe_customer_id, then exact email, then E.164 phone. It runs only while STRIPE_MATCH_REPORT_ENABLED is true (a constant, default false). getStripeMatchReport returns disabled before the role check and before any application or Stripe read. It never writes a customer id or a rental.
- Unmatched Stripe payments: one matcher in stripe-unmatched.ts. A paid invoice is linked when its subscription is on a rental, or its customer is on a rental or an application. The payments webhook logs that shape with console.warn when an invoice matches nothing, and it does not insert a row. warnUnmatchedInvoice and warnUnmatchedPayment each catch their own failure, so a log cannot fail the request. A rental read error returns without a log and without a throw. Payment-intent events do not call the extra warning. The Payments screen lists live paid invoices behind STRIPE_UNMATCHED_PAYMENTS_ENABLED, a hard-false constant that returns before auth, through the existing createReadOnlyStripe. There is no second Stripe reader.
- Collections Watch: owners and managers only, and only while COLLECTIONS_WATCH_ENABLED is true (a constant, default false). getCollectionsWatch returns disabled before the role check, driver rows, or Stripe. The reader is list/retrieve only (subscriptions, charges, invoices, and a customer, invoice, or charge a list did not expand). Upcoming invoice preview is skipped and noted, because that call is not a list or retrieve. Each row is matched to a driver by stripe-driver-match.ts using the #76 order (Stripe customer id, then exact email, then E.164 phone; more than one candidate at a tier stays unmatched). The screen shows that driver's phone and never retries a charge, sends a notice, or writes.
- Deposits & Refunds Ledger: owners and managers only, and only while DEPOSITS_LEDGER_ENABLED is true (a constant, default false). getDepositsLedger returns disabled before the role check, deposit rows, or Stripe. Refunds and gross collected are refunds.list and charges.list only; the original charge date comes from the list expand, never a retrieve. Held deposits are read from rentals and deposit payments, and a period with no held rows says "No deposit records". The screen never settles a deposit or issues a refund.
- Driver Statement: owners and managers only, on the driver Payments tab, and only while DRIVER_STATEMENT_ENABLED is true (a constant, default false). getDriverStatement returns disabled before the role check, driver rows, or Stripe. Charges, refunds, and subscription invoices are list calls; retrieve is only for a customer or charge a list did not expand. A row is included only when stripe-driver-match.ts (#76 rules) points at that driver. Download CSV is built in the browser from the loaded rows and is not stored. The screen never charges or refunds.
- Weekly Cash Flow: owners and managers, because Payments and Expenses are Manager screens. On only while WEEKLY_CASH_FLOW_ENABLED is true (a constant, default false). getWeeklyCashFlow returns disabled before the role check, vehicle_expenses, or Stripe. Gross and refunds are charges.list and refunds.list only. Weeks are Monday through Sunday, Eastern Time. A non-USD succeeded amount withholds that week and the 12-week Stripe rollup. The screen never writes.
- Shared Stripe reader: Stripe Match, Collections Watch, Deposits & Refunds, Driver Statement, Weekly Cash Flow, Partner Statement, Stripe Collections, Payout Breakdown, Billing Health, Deposit Ledger, Rent Payment Reconciliation, Stripe Disputes & Failed-Payment Monitor, Stripe Payout Reconciliation, Unmatched Stripe Payments, and Monthly Owner P&L fees list and retrieve only through src/lib/stripe-reader.server.ts. The client is a frozen facade of those two methods, so a property descriptor, prototype, own-key walk, _requestSender, or resource._stripe cannot reach rawRequest or a write. It stops at 100 pages and logs when that cap is hit, and turns HTTP 429 and timeouts into "Stripe temporarily unavailable". The 60 second query cache is opt-in. Stripe Collections leaves it off, because that screen is live and must not keep a paid or unpaid status after a payment. Checkout, subscription, and payment collection stay the only write paths. Flag defaults are unchanged, and a flag that is off returns before Stripe.
- Partner Statement: owners only, and only while PARTNER_STATEMENT_ENABLED is true (a constant, default false). getPartnerStatement returns disabled before the owner check and before any table or Stripe read while it is false. The statement lists vehicles.partner_id for one partner and one Eastern Time period (This Month, Last Month, 90 Days). Attributed revenue follows the Stripe charge rules in partner-statement.ts. charges.list goes through createReadOnlyStripe with the cache opted in, which Owner P&L shares, and any other Stripe method is refused. Unattributed charges and deposits are separate and are not in the share; another partner's vehicle is omitted. Expenses count a linked service and its expense once. Net is revenue minus expenses. Partner share is partners.revenue_split_pct of attributed revenue, or "Split not configured" when the percent is missing or unusable. CSV is built in the browser. No writes, no SQL, and no email to partners.
