- Deposit Ledger: DEPOSIT_LEDGER_ENABLED is a hard constant, false. While it is false the admin tab is absent and getDepositLedger throws before requireManager and before any deposit read, including Stripe. With the flag on, owners and managers get a read-only list from rentals, deposit payments, and deposit_deductions (expected, collected, held, due back in the next 7 Eastern Time days, or unknown). A database disagreement is evidence text only. When a payment refund and a rental refund both exist and differ, the row is Unknown and neither amount is chosen. The optional Stripe cross-check uses createReadOnlyStripe("live", { cache: true }) and only retrieves a payment intent and its charge for payments on the listed rows, matched by payments.stripe_payment_intent_id. It shows the Stripe amount captured and the amount refunded from the charge. It does not call refunds.list. A difference is an evidence sentence and does not change the row. A Stripe error shows "Stripe check unavailable" and keeps the database rows and totals. Days Since Close and the closed-only Due Back filter appear only while DEPOSIT_RETURNS_DUE_ENABLED is true (a separate hard constant, false). A closed-only request throws DEPOSIT_RETURNS_DUE_OFF before the manager check. Days use an Eastern Time calendar day: a date-only close is not shifted, and a timestamp is converted with America/New_York. No writes and no refunds.
- Rent Payment Reconciliation: owners and managers only, and only while RENT_RECONCILIATION_ENABLED is true (a constant, default false). getRentReconciliation throws before requireManager and before any payment or Stripe read while it is false; the tab is omitted and a direct link falls back to Overview. With the flag on, one Monday through Sunday week in Eastern Time (default the last full week) compares rent payments with Stripe. Deposits are excluded. The reader is createReadOnlyStripe("live", { cache: true }) and only lists charges and refunds and retrieves payment intents, charges, and invoices the database already references. Categories are matched, missing or failed in Stripe, in Stripe only for a succeeded charge on a known customer, amount mismatch, and a refund that is not reflected in net_collected. Each difference is an evidence sentence. A page cap or a Stripe error shows "Stripe check unavailable" and keeps the database rows. CSV is built in the browser. No writes, no SQL, and no Stripe configuration changes.
- Owner Weekly Finance Snapshot: owners only, and only while OWNER_FINANCE_SNAPSHOT_ENABLED is true (a constant, default false). getOwnerFinanceSnapshot throws before requireOwner and before any payment, reconciliation, or Stripe read while it is false; the tab is omitted and a direct link falls back to Overview. With the flag on, one Monday through Sunday week in Eastern Time (default the last full week) shows tiles. Rent collected is collectionSummary on the rows readPaymentReport already returns, limited to paid_date in that week; deposits stay out of that total. Reconciliation exceptions are the non-matched rows from runRentReconciliation, and only when that read's Stripe check is ready. The snapshot does not turn RENT_RECONCILIATION_ENABLED on. Stripe stays the existing cached createReadOnlyStripe list and retrieve inside Rent Payment Reconciliation. Outstanding Balance is the sum of the Receivables Aging buckets, Deposits Held and Refunds Due are the Deposit Ledger totals, and 4-Week Forecast is the Revenue Forecast total. Those reads call runReceivablesAging, runDepositLedger, and runRevenueForecast with enabled true only after the snapshot flag and the owner check, and they do not turn RECEIVABLES_AGING_ENABLED, DEPOSIT_LEDGER_ENABLED, or REVENUE_FORECAST_ENABLED on. Deposit Stripe stays readLiveDepositStripe. A warning or a withheld total says "Not available". Fleet utilization has no standalone reader, so that tile says "Not available". A failed read says "Not available" and the other tiles stay. Rent Collected links to Payments. Reconciliation Exceptions links to Rent Reconciliation. CSV is built in the browser. No writes, no SQL, and no new Stripe path.
- Tax Year Export: owners only, and only while TAX_EXPORT_ENABLED is true (a constant, default false). getTaxExport throws before requireOwner and before any payment, refund, expense, or maintenance read while it is false; the tab is omitted and a direct link falls back to Overview. Managers and coordinators are Forbidden. With the flag on, an owner picks one Eastern Time calendar year. Six CSVs are built in the browser: rental income (paid or refunded net collected, deposits excluded), deposits held at December 31, refunds, processing fees, vehicle expenses by category, and maintenance company share. Each file has a total in cents. Stripe processing fees are not stored, so every fee is Unavailable and Stripe is not called. rental.deposit_held is the current flag and is not used as a past year-end balance. Gaps are listed on the screen. No writes and no SQL.
