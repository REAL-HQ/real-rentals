- Stripe Disputes & Failed-Payment Monitor: owners and managers only, and only while STRIPE_RISK_MONITOR_ENABLED is true (a constant, default false). getStripeRiskMonitor throws before requireManager and before any driver or Stripe read while it is false; the tab is omitted and a direct link falls back to Overview. With the flag on, the reader is createReadOnlyStripe("live", { cache: true }) and only lists disputes, charges, payment intents, and subscriptions. Open disputes that need a response show the evidence due date. Failed charges and payment intents are the last 14 days and show the decline code. Subscriptions are past_due or unpaid. Each row is matched to a driver by payments.stripe_payment_intent_id, rentals.stripe_subscription_id, or a Stripe customer id on applications or rentals. More than one driver at that key stays unmatched. Times are Eastern Time. Disputes sort by the evidence deadline first. Rows link to the driver and to Payments. A Stripe error or a page cap shows "Stripe check unavailable" and withholds the rows. CSV is built in the browser. The screen never responds to a dispute, retries a payment, or writes.
- Stripe Payout Reconciliation: owners and managers only, and only while STRIPE_PAYOUT_RECON_ENABLED is true (a constant, default false). The flag gate is middleware before requireSupabaseAuth and returns before any payment or Stripe read while it is false; the tab is omitted and a direct link falls back to Overview. With the flag on, the last 60 Eastern Time days are selected by payout arrival_date. The reader is createReadOnlyStripe("live", { cache: true }). It lists payouts, balance transactions filtered by payout, and paid invoices in that window. data.source is expanded so the charge's payment_intent or its invoice id can be matched to payments.stripe_payment_intent_id or payments.stripe_invoice_id. An invoice id on a charge that the window list did not include is loaded with invoices.retrieve, including an invoice created before the window. That retrieve stops at 100 ids. The number of invoices returned must match the number requested. Fleet Link uses invoiceIsLinked on matched charges, unmatched charges, and paid rows whose payment intent is on no payout. Gross, fees, refunds, and net come from those balance transactions. A Stripe error shows "Stripe check unavailable". A page cap shows "Stripe check hit the page cap". Either one withholds the rows. CSV is built in the browser. No writes, no SQL, no payout create or cancel, and no refund create.
- Expense Categories: managers and owners, off while FINANCE_EXPENSE_CATEGORIES_ENABLED is false. getExpenseCategories returns disabled before the role check and before any table read; a direct link falls back to Overview and the tab is omitted. With the flag on, one Eastern Time period groups vehicle_expenses by category. Each expense row is counted once. A row outside the window is skipped before its amount is checked. A date that cannot be placed, an unreadable amount, or a repeated id withholds the totals. Maintenance company share is not read. CSV is built in the browser. A 100-page cap withholds the report. No Stripe, no writes, and no SQL.
- Vehicle Profit: managers and owners, a section of Expense Categories, off while VEHICLE_PNL_ENABLED is false. There is no second screen. getVehiclePnl returns disabled before the role check and before any table or Stripe read. With the flag on, one Eastern Time month shows verified revenue, expenses by category, tolls and tickets, maintenance, and net. Verified revenue uses Overview's aggregateCollections and consolidatedRevenue helpers, grouped by vehicle id, and it is not the Overview total. Overview counts Stripe deposit charges and refunds. A payment with only a Stripe invoice id is not added to Overview's non-Stripe sum (#234). A later Stripe success clears only failure_reason on that already-paid row and does not change status or paid_date, so Overview counts the money once, as Stripe. When staff took cash and Stripe also collected, the duplicate needs a refund. This report leaves those deposits out and does not add a payment that already has a Stripe payment intent or invoice id. Stripe net is after refunds and fees, dated by capture time, and a charge counts only after the same customer-ownership, settlement-amount, and bad-refund checks as Overview. The pinned Charge has no invoice field, so the invoice id is taken from the invoice payment that names the charge or its payment intent. Recorded payments with no Stripe payment intent or invoice id are added for rent, late fee, and other. A payment with a Stripe payment intent or invoice id is not added again, including when the charge was captured in another month. Toll charges are separate from expenses. Maintenance uses company share, or total cost when the share is blank, and a linked in-month expense is one cost. An unreadable amount is Unknown, never zero. Payments, expenses, tolls, and maintenance are filtered to the month and paged with limit+1. A truncated page withholds that amount. The Unattributed row includes its net. Stripe is createReadOnlyStripe without the cache, list and retrieve only, matching Overview. No writes and no SQL.
