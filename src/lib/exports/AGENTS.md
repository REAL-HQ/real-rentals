- Monthly Finance Export: managers and owners, a download on the Vehicle Profit section, off while EXPENSE_EXPORT_ENABLED is false or VEHICLE_PNL_ENABLED is false. runExpenseExport returns disabled before the role check and before any read when either constant is false. There is no new route or tab. Payments and Expenses have no CSV. Expense Categories already downloads its category totals in the browser; this file is the per-vehicle month. getExpenseExport returns disabled before the role check and before any table or Stripe read. With both flags on it calls runVehiclePnl, so revenue, expenses, tolls, maintenance, and net are that report. Unknown amounts are blank cells marked Unknown. A truncated limit+1 read writes a TRUNCATED line after the header, and the section says This Export Is Truncated. Text is always formula-guarded, including a vehicle label such as -12.34, a leading zero-width space, and full-width ＋ － ＠. Only money columns stay numbers. An Unattributed category cell is 0.00 Known when that expense total is known. The Unattributed net is the report's net. Stripe stays createReadOnlyStripe inside the Vehicle Profit read. No writes and no SQL.
- Finance Data Quality: managers and owners, off while FINANCE_DATA_QUALITY_ENABLED is false. getFinanceDataQuality throws before the role check and before any table read; a direct link falls back to Overview and the tab is omitted. With the flag on it lists payments with no method or an unmapped method, paid payments missing paid_date, refunded payments missing refunded_at or refunded_amount, card credit or debit payments missing stripe_payment_intent_id, duplicate Stripe payment ids, active rentals missing a weekly rate vehicle or driver, deposits marked held with a blank amount, and payment_refunds rows that point at missing payments. Each row has the problem, a record link, and a plain-language fix. Counts and CSV are built in the browser. A 100-page cap withholds the report. No Stripe, no writes, and no SQL.
- Rent Aging: managers and owners, off while FINANCE_AR_AGING_ENABLED is false. getRentAging throws before the role check and before any table read; a direct link falls back to Overview and the tab is omitted. With the flag on it ages outstanding payments rows, which are the invoice (due_date, balance_due, stripe_invoice_id) and the payment (paid_date, status). There is no invoices table. Deposits, void, waived, cancelled, paid, and refunded rows are not balances. Due today or later is Current; then 1–7, 8–14, 15–30, and 31 or more days as 30+. Day 30 stays in 15–30. Dates are Eastern Time calendar days, and a stored date is not shifted. Each row is one rental, or one renter when the charge has no rental: renter, rental link, amount due, oldest unpaid date, and last payment date. Bucket totals and the CSV are built in the browser. A 100-page cap, a failed read, a negative balance, or an outstanding row with no due date withholds the report. No Stripe, no writes, and no SQL.
- Deposit Ledger: managers and owners, off while FINANCE_DEPOSIT_LEDGER_ENABLED is false. getFinanceDepositLedger throws before the role check and before any table read; a direct link falls back to Overview and the tab is omitted. With the flag on, each rental with a deposit signal shows collected amount, collected date, and method from paid or refunded deposit payments, held from rentals.deposit_held and deposit_status, applied from deposit_deductions, refunded from the rental refund amount when it is set, and the balance still held only when collected, applied, and refunded are all known. The rental deposit amount is the amount required, not cash collected. No deduction row is not zero. payments.paid_date is a calendar date and is not shifted. deposit_settled_at is an Eastern Time day. Totals add only known amounts. CSV is built in the browser. A 100-page cap or a failed read withholds the report. No Stripe, no writes, and no SQL.
