# "What's Included in Your Rental" — removed from the signed agreement

**Status: NOT PUBLISHED. Not approved for any customer-facing surface.**

Agreement v1.13's PDF carried a six-card marketing panel between Section 12
and the Renter Acknowledgments. Phase A removed it from the agreement body
and the generated PDF by approved business decision: it is promotional copy,
it is not a term either party agrees to, and two of the cards make claims
that need outside verification before they appear anywhere.

The content is preserved here verbatim so the decision does not lose it. It
is **data, not a publishing instruction.** Nothing reads this file at
runtime; putting it on the website or in the Driver Portal is a separate,
explicit decision.

## Verification required before publication

| Card | What needs verifying, and by whom |
|---|---|
| **Drive for Any Platform** | Names Uber, Lyft, DoorDash and Instacart. Platform eligibility and whether a REAL RENTALS vehicle may be used on each account is the platforms' to confirm, not ours to assert. |
| **Maintenance Included** | States what REAL RENTALS pays for. Must match the maintenance obligations actually in Section 6, which puts completion on the renter and excludes damage from missed service. |
| **No Security Deposit** | Agrees with v1.13 §2 today. Re-check if deposit wording is ever approved. |
| **Repair Swaps** | §6 says a replacement is *not guaranteed*. "We'll try to swap you" is consistent only with that caveat attached. |

Any coverage or insurance implication — including putting this next to a
vehicle insurance card in the Driver Portal — needs the insurer's written
position first. v1.13 §3 says our insurance does not cover the renter.

## The six cards, as written in the v1.13 PDF

**Unlimited Miles** — Drive as much as you need within Florida. No mileage
limits or per-mile charges.

**Maintenance Included** — We pay for routine maintenance, oil changes, tires
from normal wear, and mechanical repairs at our approved vendors.

**No Security Deposit** — No deposit required. Just a valid card on file.

**Drive for Any Platform** — Uber, Lyft, DoorDash, Instacart, and other
rideshare or delivery work, plus personal driving.

**Repair Swaps** — Mechanical repair? We'll try to swap you into a comparable
vehicle. If none is available, no rent while you're without a car.

**Flexible Payments** — Pay weekly or monthly, by card autopay or an approved
payment app.
